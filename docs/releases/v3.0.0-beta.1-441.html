PortPanic 3.0.0 Beta 1

PortPanic 3.0.0 Beta 1, build 441

The first public testing release of the rebuilt PortPanic.

PortPanic lives in your Mac's menu bar. When a camera tethered to Capture One
drops, it shows a red glow and loops a sound. Reconnecting stops that alert
and gives a short green glow and recovery sound. Dismiss ends the alert while
keeping the camera's lost status visible.

Choose sounds, colors, displays and audio output in Settings. You can mute
sounds, hide the menu bar icon and test both alerts without unplugging a cable.
Update checks are optional. Camera monitoring and logs stay on your Mac.
No account or analytics.

Install
Open the DMG and drag PortPanic to Applications. Open it from Applications,
then allow Capture One access in Settings, Monitoring when needed.
Requires macOS 14 or later and Capture One 16.x. The app includes Apple silicon
and Intel builds. Most testing has been with Capture One 16.8 on Apple silicon.

Please test
Before a shoot, check Test Loss Alert, then tether your camera and pull its
cable. Check the red alert and sound, reconnect, and check that the loss sound
stops and the green recovery appears. Try Dismiss, another reconnect, and
quitting Capture One. Check Settings responsiveness on your Mac.

Still being checked
Real-camera timing and reconnect reliability across setups, an extended
shooting soak, fresh-install Automation permissions, display/audio/keyboard
behavior, macOS 14 and Intel execution. The Settings CPU correction also needs
confirmation on the affected macOS 26.3 machine.

Report problems
https://github.com/austinpresley/PortPanic-Releases/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml
Include your Mac, macOS, Capture One version, camera and cable/USB setup, what
you expected, and what happened. Settings, Monitoring, Export Diagnostics saves
a file you can review and attach. It includes device names, but no image names
or session paths.

PortPanic is free to use and share unmodified. Application source is private.
Bundled components retain their own licenses.

This is a public beta for testing. Real-camera drop/recovery, extended shooting, fresh-install permissions, and macOS 14/Intel coverage are still being checked. Test with your own setup before relying on it during a shoot.
