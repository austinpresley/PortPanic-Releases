PortPanic 3.2.0 Beta 3

Changes since 3.2.0 Beta 2:

- The menu shows camera status at a glance and puts the current action up front: Unplug, Cancel or Dismiss. The Unplug countdown shows how long you have to disconnect silently.
- Settings now has Alerts, Camera, Shortcuts and General tabs. Click an alert preview to test it, and open Camera History from the menu.
- The Loss alert shows a disconnected camera icon and your Dismiss shortcut when enabled.
- The updated PortPanic Control key shows clearer camera states and an approval countdown. To install or update the bundled Stream Deck plugin 1.0.0.473, open Settings, Shortcuts, then click Install key under Stream Deck.

macOS 14 or later. Apple silicon and Intel.

Test the alert and take a test photo in Capture One before your first shoot.

This is a public beta for testing. Real-camera drop/recovery, extended shooting, fresh-install permissions, and macOS 14/Intel coverage are still being checked. Test with your own setup before relying on it during a shoot.
